DegreePath

DegreePath is a university degree-planning tool designed to help students plan their semesters and stay on track toward graduation.

The application centralizes academic information, allowing students to track completed courses, understand remaining degree requirements, and generate personalized schedules based on prerequisites, credit loads, and scheduling preferences.

Main Features

* Degree Progress Tracking: View completed and remaining degree requirements.
* Course Recommendations: Identify eligible courses based on prerequisites and degree requirements.
* Schedule Generation: Generate and compare semester schedules based on student preferences.
* Graduation Forecasting: Estimate graduation dates and identify potential delays.
* Alternative Planning: Explore different course loads and summer classes to potentially graduate earlier.

Project Scope

The initial version will use structured academic data for a limited set of degree programs. It will focus on academic planning and recommendations rather than directly registering students for courses.

Project Goal

The goal of DegreePath is to provide students with a centralized tool that simplifies academic planning and helps them make informed decisions about their path to graduation.
